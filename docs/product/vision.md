# Vision

flust helps people quit pornography, including AI-generated content, and track their abstinence. It runs on iOS and Android and is built with Expo.

flust is the first app of fnyx-group, a non-profit open source project. The source code is released under the Mozilla Public License 2.0.

## Principles

- No account. The app works from the first launch without signing up.
- No ads, no tracking, no analytics.
- No network calls at all. The app works fully offline and never contacts a server.
- Everything is stored encrypted on the device, with keys held by the platform keystores (Keychain on iOS, Android Keystore on Android).
- The tone is calm and non-judgmental. The app never shames, scolds or pressures the user.
- A lapse is information, not failure. It helps the user understand their patterns and adjust their plan.

## Non-goals

- Social features: no friends, groups, chat or public profiles.
- Leaderboards or any comparison between users.
- Cloud sync or server-side storage.
- AI analysis of user content. The app never sends the journal, check-ins or any other content to a model, local or remote.
- Selling or sharing data. There is no data to sell, since nothing leaves the device.
